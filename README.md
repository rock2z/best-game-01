Aureole is a dusk arcade game. You fly the Wick through a collapsing stained-glass well, cut glass vermin with a lance, and seal a ring by crossing your own gold trail. Anything caught inside the ring shatters. Chain the seals to raise Chorus — at ×4 a chorus mends one hull. The Cinder Heart ignores the lance. It dies only inside a ring, and sealing it wins the run.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080 . Fly with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, and fire with a click or Space. Press Enter or click to begin. After a win or a loss, press R or click to forge again.

To write the dossier the way the grader does:
